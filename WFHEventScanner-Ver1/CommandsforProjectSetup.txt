Business Problem: Efficient Guest Check-in and Table Assignment for Large Event
An event with approximately 1,000 attendees requires a fast, accurate, and organized check-in process. Currently, managing guest entry and directing them to their assigned tables based on predefined categories (color codes) is manual, time-consuming, and prone to errors.

The event organizers maintain an Excel sheet with attendee details, including names, email addresses, and color-coded table assignments. However, there is no automated system to:

Distribute personalized entry credentials to attendees in advance

Verify attendees efficiently at the venue

Quickly identify and direct guests to the correct tables

Track which invitations have been sent and completed

Key Challenges
Scalability: Handling check-in for 1,000 attendees without long wait times or congestion at the entrance

Accuracy: Ensuring each guest is directed to the correct table based on their assigned color code

Manual Effort: Current processes require significant human involvement for sending invitations, verifying guests, and managing lists

Lack of Real-Time Validation: No system to instantly confirm attendee identity and assignment during check-in

Poor Tracking: No reliable way to track invitation delivery status or completion

Business Impact
Long queues and poor attendee experience at entry

Increased risk of misdirecting guests

Higher operational overhead due to manual coordination

Potential delays in event flow and schedule

Objective
Design and implement a streamlined, automated solution that:

Sends personalized, scannable entry credentials (barcodes) to all attendees

Enables quick and reliable check-in through barcode scanning

Instantly displays attendee details and table assignment (color code)

Tracks invitation status and completion in a centralized system

Desired Outcome
Reduce check-in time per attendee to a few seconds

Improve accuracy in guest routing

Enhance overall attendee experience

Minimize manual intervention and operational errors
